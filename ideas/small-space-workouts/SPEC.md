# Small Space Workouts — SPEC

**MVP:** User picks a difficulty (beginner/intermediate/advanced) and a duration preset (Quick ~5 min / Standard ~8 min / Extended ~12 min), then "Generate workout" assembles a 5-exercise routine from the curated library (`data.js`, `generateRoutine` in `app.js`). Exercises matching the exact difficulty are preferred and shuffled; if too few exist at that level (true for "advanced", which only has 3 tagged exercises), the pool is backfilled with the next-closest difficulty rather than repeating exercises or returning a short routine — surfaced honestly in the UI copy. After previewing the routine, "Start workout" runs a built-in timer that walks through work/rest phases (`buildTimeline`), showing the current exercise's name, muscle group, and full instructions during work phases, and a "coming up next" preview during rest. Pause/Resume and Skip controls are available mid-workout; a completion screen lists everything done.

**Out of scope for this pass:** video/GIF demonstrations of each exercise (text instructions only), audio cues/beeps for phase transitions (visual countdown only), tracking workout history over time (no persistence — each session is stand-alone), custom exercise selection (the generator is random-within-filters, not a manual builder).

**Data:** 15 original bodyweight exercises in `data.js`, all explicitly chosen to fit the "small apartment" brief from the original one-liner: no jumping, no equipment beyond an optional sturdy chair (used by 2 exercises), and low-impact/quiet enough not to disturb neighbors below (each entry has a `quietNote` explaining why). Difficulty split: 6 beginner, 6 intermediate, 3 advanced — instructions are the author's own concise write-ups of standard, well-known bodyweight exercise techniques (wall sit, plank, glute bridge, etc.), not copied from any single fitness source.
