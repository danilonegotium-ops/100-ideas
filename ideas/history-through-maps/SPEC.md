# History Through Maps — SPEC

**MVP:** Scoped down (per `MASTER_TRACKER.md`) from a full continuous 1000-year animation to 6 curated eras — 500 CE, 1200 CE, 1500 CE, 1800 CE, 1914, and 2000 — covering Europe and its immediate neighbors (Anatolia). A year slider (with clickable year labels as ticks) jumps between eras; each era shows a title, a short blurb (3-5 sentences) of what changed, an SVG schematic, and a text legend. The map is a **simplified, illustrative schematic, not real cartography**: 10 named regions (British Isles, Scandinavia, Poland/Baltic, Russia/Eastern Europe, Iberia, France/Gaul, Germany/Central Europe, Balkans, Italy, Anatolia) each sit in a fixed grid cell that never moves across eras — only the ruling power's name (and the blurb) change as the slider moves. This is stated explicitly in the page copy so nobody mistakes it for a real boundary map. A "Play through eras" button auto-advances through all 6 on a timer for a quick overview.

**Data source (see the coordinator's task note asking specifically about this):** I checked for a suitably-licensed open dataset first. The "historical-basemaps" project on GitHub (github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps) does provide real historical-boundary GeoJSON by year, fetched and confirmed reachable (`raw.githubusercontent.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps/master/geojson/world_1500.geojson` returns 200), but its `LICENSE` file is **GPLv3** — not CC0/public-domain, and GPL's copyleft terms are a bad fit to embed as a static asset in this otherwise-unlicensed project. Rather than risk that, I went with the explicitly-sanctioned fallback: a hand-built, illustrative version. The historical content itself (who held roughly which region in each era) is hand-written from general, widely-known European history (fall of Rome 476, Fourth Crusade 1204, fall of Constantinople 1453, Reconquista completed 1492, Poland's partitions 1772-95, German/Italian unification 1861-71, WWI's 1914 outbreak, the USSR's 1991 dissolution, German reunification 1990, Yugoslavia's 1990s breakup) — the kind of high-level facts in any general history overview, simplified into one paragraph per era, not sourced from a single reference or dataset.

**Out of scope for this pass:** geographically accurate borders/shapes (explicitly out of scope — see above); a continuous scrubber with in-between years (fixed 6-era stops only, per the scope note); regions outside Europe/Anatolia (no Asia, Americas, Africa beyond the immediate Mediterranean context); city-level detail (only broad political entities per region, not individual cities).

**Data/content:** `data.js` holds `REGIONS` (10 fixed schematic grid positions + a color per slot) and `ERAS` (6 eras, each with a year, title, blurb, and a ruler name per region) — both hand-authored for this project, with the sourcing/licensing reasoning documented in a comment at the top of `data.js` as well as here.
