# Sleep Sound Generator — SPEC

**MVP:** Four ambient soundscapes — Mountain forest, Rain on a city roof, Serbian village night, Ocean waves — synthesized entirely client-side with the Web Audio API (noise-buffer generators run through biquad filters, plus a few low detuned oscillators for drones and LFO-modulated filter/gain for wind and wave motion). There are no audio files anywhere in the project: `generateWhiteNoise`/`generateBrownNoise`/`generatePinkNoise` in `app.js` build raw `Float32Array` sample data that's copied into an `AudioBuffer` at play time, so there's nothing to license or host. Only one soundscape plays at a time; clicking a card's Play button fades the previous one out and the new one in (linear gain ramps, ~0.5-1.5s) to avoid audio clicks/pops. A master volume slider (0-100%) controls a single master `GainNode`. A sleep timer (15/30/45/60/90 minutes, or Off) counts down against an absolute end timestamp (`Date.now()`-based, not tick-counted, so it stays accurate even if the tab is backgrounded) and fades the sound out automatically when it reaches zero.

**Out of scope for this pass:** layering multiple soundscapes simultaneously (one at a time only, keeps the UI and the audio-graph bookkeeping simple); saving a favorite/last-played sound across sessions (no `localStorage` used here — every visit starts fresh); a custom/arbitrary timer length (fixed presets only); recording/exporting the generated audio.

**Data/content:** `data.js` holds only display metadata (name + one-line description per soundscape) — the actual sound design (frequencies, filter cutoffs, noise colors, chirp timing) lives in `app.js` as the synthesis code itself, not as external content. The soundscapes are described as "Mountain forest" / "Rain on a city roof" / "Serbian village night" / "Ocean waves" for evocative labeling per the original idea, even though they're synthesized rather than field recordings — this is stated plainly in the page copy ("generated live in your browser").
