Solar Panel ROI Calculator

For Serbian homeowners to see if solar panels are worth it based on local electricity prices.

Roughly 6 m² of usable roof per 1 kW installed, accounting for panel spacing and orientation.

If your bill is in RSD, divide by roughly 117 to get EUR.

⚠ These are rough estimates for planning only, not a formal quote. Real costs depend on your roof, equipment choice, and installer; real savings depend on your actual consumption pattern and EPS's current tiered tariffs.
